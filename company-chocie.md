I am most interested in developing an AI-powered Customer Experience Agent that automates repetitive customer inquiries, particularly shipment tracking questions, delivery updates, and return-status requests. TrackFlow's briefing states that approximately 80% of customer queries could be answered automatically. I believe this is an excellent opportunity to use AI, RAG, and workflow automation to reduce manual work and improve customer satisfaction.




## My AI Agent Idea

I propose building an AI-powered Customer Experience Agent for TrackFlow.

The agent will use a RAG-based knowledge base containing official company documentation and connect to tracking and returns systems to retrieve relevant information. It will answer common customer questions about shipments, delivery updates, and returns.

If the agent cannot find reliable information or encounters a complicated issue, it will escalate the conversation to a human support representative.

The goal is to reduce repetitive manual work, improve response times, provide support outside regular business hours, and allow human agents to focus on more complex customer issues.
