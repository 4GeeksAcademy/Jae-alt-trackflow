# TrackFlow — Company Briefing

## Company Overview

TrackFlow is a last-mile delivery and warehouse management company founded in 2009. It operates in the United States and Spain, with warehouses in Los Angeles and Zaragoza.

The company provides inventory storage, order fulfillment, shipping, carrier management, and returns processing for e-commerce brands.

TrackFlow employs approximately 130 people and generates around €9 million in annual revenue.

## Selected Department: Customer Experience

**Department Manager:** Valentina Cruz

**Team Size:** 15 agents across Los Angeles and Zaragoza.

### Current Challenges

- Customer support is handled through email, WhatsApp, and phone without a unified ticketing system.
- Approximately 80% of customer queries could potentially be answered automatically.
- There is no centralized knowledge base.
- Customer support is unavailable outside office hours.
- Agents handle both business clients (B2B) and end consumers (B2C).

### Business Needs

- An AI first-line customer support agent.
- Automatic responses to shipment tracking questions.
- Automated return-status inquiries.
- A semantic knowledge base using RAG.
- A unified ticketing system.
- Sentiment analysis to identify frustrated customers.
- Spanish and English language support.

## Proposed AI Solution

Develop an AI-powered Customer Experience Agent that helps automate repetitive customer inquiries and improves response times.

The agent will use a RAG-based knowledge base to retrieve accurate information from official company documentation. It will also connect to shipment tracking and returns systems to provide relevant customer updates.

When a question is too complex, sensitive, or requires human intervention, the agent will escalate the conversation to a customer support representative.

## Expected Business Benefits

- Reduce repetitive manual customer support work.
- Improve response times.
- Provide customer assistance outside office hours.
- Deliver more consistent answers.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
- Allow human agents to focus on complex customer issues.
